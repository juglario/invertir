The School

What comes after the basics.

Fifteen in-depth entries, with simulators and charts to understand each idea. They go from easier to harder: pick one and give it the time it asks for.

How to read this

Four levels. One entry at a time.

Each entry explains an idea that experienced investors use, with real examples, simulators to try it with your own numbers and an exercise to do this week. They assume you know what a stock, a bond and an index fund are: if you have not seen the twelve lessons of Investing from Zero yet, start there. Each entry tells you which lesson to have fresh in mind.
